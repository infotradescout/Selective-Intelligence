A straightforward approach

Clarity before commitment.

A repair conversation should separate what is known from what still needs to be checked.

  1. 01

    Describe

    Share the item, the change you noticed, and any pattern you can observe safely.

  2. 02

    Confirm

    Use the first conversation to confirm whether the job is a fit and what information is still needed.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Agree on the next step only after the scope and any open questions are understood.
