Start with the problem

Not every repair needs the same kind of help.

Instead of making you choose a service before you know what is wrong, the request begins with what you can observe.

01

Something is loose

A hinge, handle, fitting, leg, bracket, or other part no longer feels secure.

02

Something will not move

A drawer, door, latch, wheel, or mechanism is stuck, dragging, or out of line.

03

?

Something just seems off

A sound, smell, wobble, leak, or change is telling you the issue needs a closer look.

Not sure where it fits? Describe what you notice. Scope and availability should always be confirmed before scheduling.

A low-pressure process

Clarity before commitment.

Three simple steps keep the first conversation focused on the repair—not a sales pitch.

  1. 01

    Describe what you see

    Share the item or area, the change you noticed, and anything you have already tried.

  2. 02

    Confirm the fit

    The repair, service area, timing, and any information still needed are clarified first.

  3. 03

    Agree on the next step

    Only then should a visit, estimate, referral, or safe alternative be discussed.
