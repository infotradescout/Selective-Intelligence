04 / Know before you go

        
Useful questions, answered plainly.

      

      

        

          Can this page give me a repair price?
          
No. The item, problem, and likely work need to be understood first. This draft deliberately avoids invented prices and estimates.

        

        

          Where does my form information go?
          
Nowhere. The page formats your answers inside this browser tab. Nothing is transmitted or stored; copy the brief if you want to keep it.

        

        

          What should I avoid including?
          
Do not add passwords, access codes, payment details, or other sensitive information. Item details and a plain description of the problem are enough to begin.

        

        

          What if the item seems unsafe?
          
Stop using it. If there is smoke, sparking, leaking, unusual heat, a gas odor, or immediate danger, move away and contact the appropriate local emergency or qualified service.
